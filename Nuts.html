Cup Nut

The Cup Nut is a compact and highly durable fastening solution. Its round body allows for secure tightening and reliable performance in scaffolding and shuttering systems.

  • Material: High-grade cast steel
  • Features: Accurate threading, corrosion-resistant finish
  • Use: Suitable for heavy-duty tie rod connections
Jack Nut

Our Jack Nut is specifically designed for scaffolding jacks, ensuring smooth adjustment and excellent load support. It helps in achieving accurate leveling during construction.

  • Thread Type: Precision-cut for easy movement
  • Strength: Heavy load capacity
  • Finish: Painted / Powder-coated
Prop Nut (L&T Type)

The L&T Prop Nut is widely used in adjustable props for supporting formwork at different heights. Built for durability, it offers excellent adjustability and reliability.

  • Material: Cast Iron / SG Iron
  • Finish: Painted or Galvanized Coating
  • Application: Construction props and support systems
Prop Nut

A versatile and essential scaffolding component, the Prop Nut allows for quick and secure adjustment of scaffolding props.

  • Features: Rugged design, smooth operation
  • Load Bearing: High capacity
  • Finish: Anti-rust coating for longer life
Wing Nut

The Wing Nut is designed for easy tightening and loosening of tie rods by hand. Its ergonomic wing shape makes it fast and convenient to use without tools.

  • Material: Malleable cast iron
  • Finish: Painted
  • Use: Formwork tie rod assembly
Anchor Nut

The Anchor Nut is designed for use with tie rods in formwork and scaffolding applications. With its solid casting and excellent load-bearing capacity, it guarantees strong and stable joints.

  • Material: Ductile / Malleable Cast Iron
  • Finish: Painted / Galvanized
  • Application: Tie rod and formwork system

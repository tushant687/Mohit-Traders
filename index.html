What We Make

At Mohit Traders, we are a trusted manufacturer of premium-quality MS Scaffolding Systems, MS Shuttering Products, MS Road Safety Railings, MS Road Safety Barriers, and Efficient support for all construction and industrial projects.

With advanced manufacturing capabilities and extensive industry experience, we deliver precision-engineered products that combine strength, safety, durability, and reliability. Our comprehensive product range includes scaffolding accessories, scaffolding nuts, shuttering plates, road safety barriers, crash barriers, pedestrian railings, highway safety structures, and customized steel fabrication products designed to meet the demanding requirements of construction and infrastructure projects.

Our commitment to quality craftsmanship, timely delivery, and customer satisfaction has made Mohit Traders a trusted partner for builders, contractors, infrastructure developers, and government projects across India.

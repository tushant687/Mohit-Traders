Advanced Manufacturing Facility for MS Scaffolding, MS Shuttering, MS Road Safety Products

Manufacturing Facility & Infrastructure

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is equipped with advanced machinery, modern fabrication equipment, and skilled professionals to produce high-quality scaffolding systems, road safety products, industrial safety railings, barricades, storage cages, and custom MS manufacturing solutions. Every product is manufactured with precision to ensure superior strength, durability, and long-lasting performance.

  • ✔️ Hydraulic Press Machines – Used for shaping and forming steel components with high precision and consistency.
  • ✔️ Welding & Fabrication Units – Modern MIG, ARC, and CO₂ welding equipment for strong, reliable, and durable steel structures.
  • ✔️ Cutting & Drilling Machines – Precision cutting, drilling, and fabrication for customized project requirements.
  • ✔️ Thread Rolling & Finishing Machines – High-quality threaded components for scaffolding systems and industrial applications.
  • ✔️ Powder Coating & Surface Finishing – Protective coating that enhances corrosion resistance, durability, and product life.
  • ✔️ Quality Inspection – Every product undergoes strict quality checks to ensure safety, accuracy, and compliance with industry standards.

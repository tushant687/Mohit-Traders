Our Scaffolding & Shuttering Products

Building with confidence starts here. We provide premium scaffolding and shuttering solutions that combine strength, safety, and durability to meet the toughest construction demands. MS Challi

The MS Challi is a high-strength steel mesh shuttering accessory used in formwork systems for better grip and enhanced bonding of concrete.

  • Improves concrete bonding & grip
  • Durable & reusable multiple times
  • Ensures smooth surface finishing
MS Shuttering Plate

The MS Shuttering Plate is a robust steel plate used as a base in concrete slab casting, ensuring uniform finishing and high load capacity.

  • Heavy load-bearing strength
  • Rust & corrosion resistant coating
  • Cost-effective with multiple reuse
MS Bracket Shuttering Plate

The MS Bracket Shuttering Plate provides strong support in beam and slab formwork, maintaining stability and alignment during construction.

  • Strong and stable support system
  • Easy to fix & dismantle
  • Anti-rust surface for long life
MS Haunch Shuttering Plate

The MS Haunch Shuttering Plate is widely used in bridges and flyovers for haunch casting, designed to withstand heavy concrete pressure.

  • Capable of handling heavy concrete loads
  • Accurate shape & dimensional stability
  • Highly durable & reusable
MS Crash Barrier Shuttering Plate

The MS Crash Barrier Shuttering Plate is designed for casting strong road & highway crash barriers with precise dimensions and durability.

  • Accurate design for perfect casting
  • High strength under heavy loads
  • Quick & easy installation on site
MS Column Plates

The MS Column Plates are specially designed for RCC vertical columns, ensuring smooth finishing and perfect alignment.

  • Strong welding joints for stability
  • Economical & reusable
  • Easy handling & placement at site
MS Round / Circular Column Shuttering

The MS Round / Circular Column Shuttering is crafted for circular RCC columns, delivering accuracy, durability, and easy handling.

  • Perfect round shape casting
  • Strong & long-lasting material
  • Quick fixing & removal system
